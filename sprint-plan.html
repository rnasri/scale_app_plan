Cross-track dependencies

Raghib → needs from Uday

  • Sales Incentives (Sep 8–15) — Commission calculation logic needs Accounts payment DocTypes. Uday reviews the commission payout posting.
  • Collections (Aug 3–5) — reads AR aging from Accounts. Chart of Accounts must be live before Collections can be tested end-to-end.

Raghib → needs from Vandana

  • PMO Invoicing — project-linked Sales Invoice depends on scale_sales Customer master. Coordinate naming convention.

Vandana → needs from Uday

  • PMO Invoicing / Expense (Aug 19–24) — billing entries post to GL. Chart of Accounts and Cost Centre setup must be complete first.
  • Payroll (Aug 31) — salary journal entries post to Accounts. Tax accounts (PF, ESI, TDS) must exist in CoA before payroll can run.

All tracks → need F0

  • F0 must be complete by Jul 23. Any slip delays all three tracks simultaneously.
  • Dev environments must be identical — use the same bench version + requirements.txt.

High-risk items — watch list

Meta API (Raghib)
IG & FB, WhatsApp, Social Media Management all depend on Meta Business API approval. Start the app review process in Sprint 0 — it can take 1–2 weeks. Blocking risk for Sprint 1–2 Marketing.

Chart of Accounts (Uday)
Rated High risk because legacy CoA migration + GST account mapping is client-specific. Sprint 0 day 1 item. Slip here delays AR/AP, Bank Recon, and PMO Invoicing in other tracks.

Sales Incentives (Raghib)
Multi-tier retrospective slabs rated High risk. Needs business rules locked in writing before Sep 8. Formula sign-off from Uday (for payment posting) needed by Sep 5.
