Sales module — scale_sales app

19 features across 3 priority tiers (P1/P2/P3). Source: SCALE ERP Modules Scope & Capability workbook — Sales tab, revised set (rows 19–37). P1 items run Jul 24 – Aug 20, P2 items Aug – Sep 15, P3 items (Win Probability, Sales Coaching) follow after Sep 15.

Frappe & ERPNext apps involved

Frappe CRM

Primary CRM UI. Provides the modern pipeline kanban, lead management, contact management, and activity logging. Install separately alongside ERPNext.

Use Frappe CRM for the sales team's daily interface. ERPNext Selling handles the transactional documents (Quotation, SO, Invoice).

ERPNext Selling

Handles Quotations, Sales Orders, Delivery Notes, and Sales Invoices. Also manages Customer master, Pricing Rules, Sales Person targets, and Sales Partner (channel partners).

The transactional spine — all revenue transactions flow through here to Accounts.

scale_sales (our app)

Built by the team. Contains all custom extensions: BANT fields, Dealer Profile DocType, Field Force Activity DocType, commission calculator, follow-up automation, and all custom reports.

This is the only app we modify. Never touch Frappe CRM or ERPNext Selling source.

Marketing module — scale_sales app

Marketing has 33 features and the largest gap from OOTB Frappe. The core strategy is: GentooLeads (in-house) handles WhatsApp, IG/FB, and campaign execution. Frappe CRM handles lead ingestion and attribution. scale_sales is the connector and analytics layer.

Wave 1 — implementation sequence

Order matters. Build in this sequence to avoid blocked dependencies:

Sales sequence (~10–12 weeks)

  1. 1

    Weeks 1–2: Core masters

    Customer DocType custom fields. Item master setup. Frappe CRM pipeline stage configuration. Assignment rules. Naming series.

  2. 2

    Weeks 2–3: Lead management

    BANT fields on Lead. Duplicate detection Server Script. Meta/Google webhook receivers. Lead Source attribution. Frappe CRM UI walkthrough.

  3. 3

    Weeks 3–4: Pipeline

    Opportunity stages config. Stakeholder & Competitor child tables. Probability auto-calc Client Script. Stale deal alert scheduler.

  4. 4

    Weeks 4–5: Transactional flow

    Quotation → Sales Order → Delivery Note → Sales Invoice end-to-end. Pricing rules. GST configuration. Print format.

  5. 5

    Weeks 5–6: Targets & Commission

    Sales Person target extension. Activity target aggregation job. Commission Rule DocType. Commission Entry on invoice submit.

  6. 6

    Weeks 6–8: Dealer & Channel

    Dealer Profile DocType. Field Force Activity DocType. Field Force mobile page (Frappe UI). Channel Partner portal. Scorecard job.

  7. 7

    Weeks 8–10: Reports & Dashboard

    Pipeline Health, Target vs Actual, Sales Forecast (weighted), Collections Aging. Sales dashboard page with KPI tiles.

  8. 8

    Weeks 10–12: QA + gate

    End-to-end UAT walkthrough. CI passing. Permissions verified per role. Raghib sign-off. Wave 1 Sales closed.

Marketing sequence (~8–10 weeks, parallel)

  1. 1

    Weeks 1–2: GentooLeads connector

    AIWS Marketing Settings DocType (API key, sync interval). Campaign sync job. Lead ingestion from GL into Frappe Lead. Smoke test: one campaign end-to-end.

  2. 2

    Weeks 2–3: Paid channel webhooks

    Meta Lead Ads webhook receiver. Google Ads UTM parser. Lead deduplication check against S-02 script. Source attribution fields.

  3. 3

    Weeks 3–4: Email marketing

    Email Campaign configuration. Mailing list management. Open/click tracking custom fields. Unsubscribe flow. Bounce handling.

  4. 4

    Weeks 4–5: Content & Assets

    Content Calendar Entry DocType. Brand Asset DocType. Calendar view configuration. Content approval workflow.

  5. 5

    Weeks 5–6: Campaign management

    Frappe CRM Campaign extension. Budget fields. Ad Spend Log DocType. SEO Report Entry DocType (GSC API import).

  6. 6

    Weeks 6–8: Analytics

    Lead Source Attribution report. Campaign ROI report. Marketing Funnel report. Marketing Head dashboard page.

  7. 7

    Weeks 8–10: QA + gate

    GentooLeads sync tested with real campaigns. Attribution verified. Raghib sign-off. Wave 1 Marketing closed.
