✅ Recently Landed — week of 1–4 Sep 2026

PMO
Predictive & Anomaly Alerts, Phase 1 complete — all 10 alerts live. The PMO assistant now proactively surfaces problems instead of waiting to be asked: a task slipping behind schedule or blocking others, spend on a trajectory to overrun budget, an outlier expense claim, a resource sitting idle or over-committed, the same risk wording recurring across projects, and more. Every alert is a deterministic check against real data, never a model guess — the assistant picks at most one to surface per turn, based on what was actually asked. Phase 2 (pushing these proactively, not just on request) is intentionally on hold pending a decision on where they should land.
integration/pmo-predictive-alerts-phase1 → main, PR #56
HR
Geo-fenced + face-verified attendance check-in. A mobile-friendly check-in page validates the employee's GPS location against their assigned site(s) and, where enabled, matches a live selfie against their enrolled photo before logging attendance — both checks are configurable per-company (off by default, warn/fail-closed/pending-review modes). A new Attendance Compliance report gives HR one place to see who checked in where, and how confidently.
feature/hrm → main, PR #55
PMO
Two real budget bugs fixed, plus the Kickoff section polish from ET-05. Project Budget actuals were going stale — a Timesheet or Expense Claim submitting against a project didn't refresh the budget unless someone happened to re-save it (6 of 19 real budgets were understated, by up to ₹36,000 in one case). Over-allocation warnings were also firing on stagings that never actually overlapped on the same day. Both fixed and verified against real records, not just unit tests. Riding along: Project Manager now displays by name everywhere, not employee ID; the Kickoff & Governance section is reorganized into paired rows; Project Code now auto-generates.
PR #54 (closes GitHub issues #52, #53)
AI · Cross-Module
PMO's Scale Intelligence assistant, next phase. Document-based project creation (draft a project from an uploaded brief), comparable-project scoring for the AI-assisted setup suggestion, role-scoped chat access, and the assistant can now show multiple suggestions/actions/follow-ups in one turn instead of just one.
PR #47
HR
Employee engagement — pulse surveys and communities. Recurring pulse surveys go out via a guest magic-link portal (no login needed to respond), employee communities get their own activity report, and there's a new Engagement workspace card plus extended CHRO KPI reporting.
PR #48
Sales & Marketing
