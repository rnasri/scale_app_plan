🏭 Bramhanee — Parallel Implementation

Stores plan → Purchase plan → Manufacturing plan →
✅ This Week (17–25 Sep)
  • A second, parallel client implementation (Archit & Aryan), running alongside the Service/SVE work above — same three apps (scale_stock, scale_purchase, scale_manufacturing), different client build.
  • Phase 1 — Store Inward. Purchase User creates the PO, Purchase Manager approves it (no self-approve — the approval queue is the POs Pending Approval report). The GRN (Purchase Receipt) is made from the PO, searchable by PO name. Partial receipt splits good qty into Stores and defective qty into Rejected Stock with a defect note; Quality Inspection pulls the PR's qty/rate/warehouse/PO. Over-receipt is blocked, stock can never go negative, a Putaway rule routes stock to the rack, Purchase Return works, and the Stock Ledger shows Out Qty as a positive number.
  • Phase 2 — Store to Shopfloor. A Material Request (Material Transfer) moves stock from Stores to WIP, routed through the approval workflow with a stock-availability check before transfer. Stock Entry executes the move and the Stock Ledger proves it (Out from Stores, In to WIP). If stock is short, a Purchase Material Request is raised instead.
  • Manufacturing & subcontracting work (Aryan): zero valuation rates now supported for service-only items (e.g. Powder Coating) without blocking workflows, with subcontracting service items auto-linked to the correct cost accounts. The Purchase Order warehouse dropdown is filtered to valid "Virtual" warehouses only. A dedicated Gate Pass print format standardizes documentation for raw materials sent to external vendors. A new "Scale Manufacturing Automations" settings section replaces hardcoded scripts with a toggle-based configuration, starting with two toggles: automatic Quality Inspection drafting on raw-material receipt (respecting item-level Inspection Required flags), and a 1-click Repack workflow directly from an approved Purchase Receipt.
  • On branch bramhanee (Phase 1 committed 18 Sep), no PR yet.
🔜 Next Up
  • Bramhanee branch review/PR planning, once both Archit's and Aryan's work is consolidated.
