Worth Knowing Before The Walkthrough

Two Real Problems Caught Before They Reached Anyone

Caught During Testing, Fixed Before Anyone Saw Them
  • A blind stock count that could silently look "counted" when it wasn't. A zero physical count and an un-entered count looked identical on save — fixed with a separate check so a real zero-count is trusted but a skipped row blocks submission.
  • A disposal request that could ask to write off more stock than actually exists. Now blocked immediately when raised, with a clear reason, instead of failing later with a confusing system error.

STR-09

Slow & Non-moving Stock

🟢 Progressed
Someone decides — discount, return, or write off — with an approval trail.
Added
  • A new Stock Disposition Request — Stores raises it, Finance approves or rejects it
  • Approving auto-posts the actual stock issue; rejecting leaves stock completely untouched
  • Blocked outright if someone tries to dispose of more than what's actually on the shelf
Pending
  • Downstream accounting treatment (write-off expense vs. vendor credit note, etc.) happens separately in Accounts, not inside this request
