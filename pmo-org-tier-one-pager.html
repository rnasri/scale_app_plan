Scale — One Login Framework, Every Client's Org Chart

Project Management Module · Foundation Overview
Our strategy: Scale is built by extending Frappe/ERPNext — the world's most widely deployed open-source ERP — with our own purpose-built apps, rather than building an ERP from the ground up. Every client organization has a different org chart and different job titles, so instead of customizing the software per client, we are standardizing on nine generic seniority tiers, each with its own purpose-built dashboard. Onboarding a new client becomes a one-time mapping exercise — matching that client's own job titles onto these nine tiers — not new software. The table below shows this worked out in full for Project Management, our first module; the same approach extends next to HR and every other Scale module.
Tier Typical Client Job Titles Dashboard What They See What They Can Approve
1Executive Developer, Site Engineer, QA Engineer, Design Engineer, Technician, Associate "My Work" hub LIVE Their own assigned Tasks across every Project (status, priority, due date — updated inline); a weekly Timesheet grid to log hours; a quick way to raise a Material Request for their Project. Nothing beyond their own work. Nothing — requester only
2Senior Executive Senior Developer, Site Supervisor, Team Lead, Senior Engineer, Shift Supervisor "My Team" hub PLANNED Everything Tier 1 sees for themselves, plus a live view of their direct reports' Tasks and Timesheets — who's overloaded, whose hours are missing, what's overdue — using each employee's own manager already recorded in their HR profile. Nothing outside their own small team. Team's Timesheets & Material Requests
3Assistant Manager Assistant Manager, Deputy Manager, Project Coordinator "My Team" hub, widened PLANNED Same view as Tier 2, but rolled up across every Supervisor's team that reports up to them — several teams side-by-side, not just one. Same categories, wider scope
4Manager Project Manager, Site Manager, Delivery Manager, Engineering Manager "My Projects" portfolio LIVE A live, colour-coded (red/amber/green) health status for every Project they run — calculated automatically from real task delays, not self-reported — alongside start/end dates, % complete, and open/overdue task counts per Project. A dedicated Approvals tile surfaces Budget sign-offs, Change Requests, and Timesheet reviews waiting on them, scoped to only the Projects they personally manage. This view already works well today — no gap found; nothing further needed at this tier. Project Budget, Change Requests, Timesheet review
5Senior Manager Senior Manager, Program Manager, Regional Manager Portfolio, rolled up PLANNED The same portfolio view as Tier 4, consolidated across every Manager and Project within one function — a "manager of managers" summary rather than one more single-project list. Higher-value approvals
6AVP AVP, Associate Director, Deputy GM, Head of Delivery Cross-department view PLANNED Health, budget, and delivery trend compared side-by-side across every department under them — spotting which department needs attention without opening each one. Value-threshold approvals
7VP VP, Director, General Manager, Head of Operations Executive Dashboard LIVE (broadened) Company-wide operational numbers for every department at a glance, plus an escalation queue for issues that couldn't be resolved lower down. A working, decision-making view — not just a report. Escalations
8President / C-Suite President, CEO, COO, CFO, CTO, Managing Director Executive Dashboard, trimmed PLANNED Company-wide strategic summary only — revenue, delivery health, and risk at a glance. Deliberately no task-level or project-level detail to click into — built by simplifying the Manager/VP views down, not adding anything new. Strategic sign-off only
9Board of Directors Board Members, Promoters, Chairman Governance scorecard PLANNED The lightest view in the system — a quarterly, portfolio-level scorecard for board meetings. No day-to-day operational access at all; likely a simpler, read-only login rather than the full system. None operational
Job titles above are illustrative examples, not a fixed list — at client onboarding, we map that client's actual job titles onto these nine tiers once. Tiers 1 and 4 are already built and live today; the rest are the next phase of this same framework. Full technical detail: Scale Org-Tier Framework — Foundation Document.
