PMO Working Manual — Roles, Logins & Workflows

Who touches the PMO module, how each person logs in, and exactly what happens at every approval step. Written for the people who will actually use scale_project_management day to day — PMO leadership, project managers, finance approvers, consultants, and clients — not for developers. For the build-vs-spec detail behind each feature, see pmo-module.html; for a live click-through, see pmo-demo-showcase.html.

1 · Roles & Logins

Two kinds of access exist: Desk — a full Frappe backend login (username/password, inside the company's ERP instance) for internal staff, and Portal — a lighter external login (email-based, website-only) for clients and resources who never see the desk. A third, Public, needs no account at all — used only for the two open web forms (consultant self-signup, NPS survey).

PMO Director

Desk
Who
The senior PMO leadership role — final sign-off authority across the module.
How they get in
Standard Frappe desk login. The PMO Director Role itself is created automatically the first time the module is bootstrapped on a site (see §4) — a System Manager then assigns it to the relevant person's User record.
What they do
Approve/reject Project Kickoff; approve/reject/deactivate Consultant Profiles; final ("Management") approver on the 4-level Project Budget chain; reads the PMO Executive Dashboard and PMO Cockpit Dashboard.

Projects Manager

Desk
Who
The day-to-day operational owner of each project — native ERPNext role, not PMO-specific.
How they get in
Standard Frappe desk login, Projects Manager role assigned by a System Manager.
What they do
Creates/edits Projects and submits them for Kickoff Review; reviews Consultant Profile applications before PMO Director sign-off; drafts and submits Project Budgets (step 1 of 4); manages Tasks, Gantt, Risk Register, Change Requests; reviews/approves timesheets day to day.

Department Head

Desk
Who
PMO-specific custom role — the second checkpoint on any project budget.
How they get in
Standard Frappe desk login, Department Head role assigned by a System Manager (this role ships as a fixture — it's on any site the moment the app is installed).
What they do
Reviews and approves/rejects a Project Budget after the Projects Manager submits it, before it reaches Finance.

Accounts Manager (Finance)

Desk
Who
Native ERPNext finance role — the third checkpoint on the budget chain.
How they get in
Standard Frappe desk login, Accounts Manager role — typically already assigned via the Accounts/Finance modules, not something PMO grants on its own.
What they do
Reviews and approves/rejects a Project Budget after the Department Head, before it goes to the PMO Director for final Management sign-off.

Employee

Desk or Portal
Who
Internal staff logging time and expenses against Projects.
How they get in
Full desk login for anyone with normal ERP access, or the mobile-friendly /timesheet-entry Web Form (still needs a Frappe User account — login_required=1 — just no desk UI). A System Manager must create the User; approving a Consultant or hiring an Employee does not auto-create one.
What they do
Logs hours against Project/Task/Activity Type, submits for the weekly manday guardrail, receives the missing-timesheet reminder if hours go unlogged.

Consultant / Contractor

Public application Desk once approved
Who
External resources — Consultants self-apply; Contractors are desk-created only (no public signup form exists for Contractor).
How they get in
A prospective Consultant applies via the public /consultant-signup Web Form — no login needed to apply. Once PMO Director-approved, they still have no automatic system access: if they need to log their own timesheets via /timesheet-entry, a System Manager has to separately create a Frappe User for them. Both Consultant and Contractor onboarding can be switched off entirely per PMO Settings (off by default on a fresh site — see §5).
What they do
Submit profile, skills, BGV/NDA docs, rate card, and weekly availability through the signup form; once approved, become bookable in Resource Allocation and (if a User exists) can log hours.

Client (Customer Portal User)

Portal
Who
The client organization's own contact(s) — never has desk access.
How they get in
A native Frappe portal login, granted when their Contact/User is added to the Customer record's Portal Users table — that add fires an automatic welcome email pointing them to /client-kickoff-pack.
What they do
Views the Client Kickoff Pack (onboarding guide, document repository, escalation contacts); approves or rejects Change Requests at /change-request-approval — this is a client action, not a PMO one; receives the automatic post-completion NPS survey email.

Anyone (Anonymous)

Public, no login
Who
Not a system user at all.
How they get in
Two specific public forms need no account: /consultant-signup (see above) and /project-nps-survey (client feedback at project close — the emailed link works for a logged-out recipient, and the form is also open for anyone with the URL).
Deliberate design choice, not an oversight: an NPS survey gated behind a login would suppress genuine response rates.

System Manager

Desk (Admin)
Who
IT/technical administrator — not a PMO business role, but the one who makes every other role possible.
How they get in
The site's own Administrator account, or any User granted System Manager.
What they do
Runs the one-time bootstrap (§4) on a fresh site, creates User accounts for staff/consultants who need logins, assigns the roles above, flips the Consultant/Contractor toggles in PMO Settings.

2 · Approval Workflows

Three are formal Frappe Workflows (a document literally cannot skip a state); five more are single-step script actions gated by desk buttons rather than a multi-state engine. Both kinds are real approval gates — the difference is just how many states the document passes through.

Project Kickoff Approval

Frappe Workflow · doctype: Project
Gate: a new Project cannot go operationally Active until PMO signs off. Native status stays locked to Open until this workflow reaches PMO Approved.
1
Projects Manager Draft
Creates the Project, completes the Kickoff Checklist, Deliverables Checklist, and role-based budget, then clicks Submit for Kickoff Review.
2
System Kickoff Pending
Approval is blocked until every Kickoff Checklist item is ticked — enforced in code, not just convention.
3
PMO Director Approve / Reject
Approve → PMO Approved, the Project can now proceed. Reject → sent back to Draft for revision.

Consultant Approval

Frappe Workflow · doctype: Consultant Profile
Gate: an applicant is not bookable on any project until PMO reviews them.

Optional, off by default: this entire workflow can be switched off. Enable Consultant Management and Enable Contractor Management are two independent toggles on PMO Settings — a client that doesn't use external consultants or contractors simply leaves the relevant one off. While off: the public /consultant-signup form auto-unpublishes and no new Consultant (or Contractor) Profile can be created at all, desk or portal; existing profiles stay fully visible and editable either way, so switching back on later needs no data recovery. Configured at Home → Scale Project Management → PMO Settings, e.g. scale.localhost:8000/app/pmo-settings on this dev bench. Both ship off on a fresh site (owner decision, 12 Aug 2026) — see §4 step 6.

1
Prospective Consultant Public form
Applies via /consultant-signup — no account needed. Lands in Pending Review.
2
PMO Director Approve / Reject
Approve → Approved, now bookable in Resource Allocation. Reject → Rejected (can be moved back to Pending Review for reconsideration).
3
PMO Director Deactivate
An Approved consultant can later be moved to Inactive — profile stays intact, just drops out of new bookings.

Project Budget Approval

Frappe Workflow · doctype: Project Budget
The one 4-level chain in the module — matches the PDF's Planning→Manager→Finance→Management flow exactly.
1
Projects Manager Draft → Pending PM Approval
Builds the 7-category budget (Labor, Materials, Procurement, Contractors, Travel, Equipment, Misc) and submits.
2
Projects Manager → Pending Dept Head Approval
Self-approves the PM checkpoint (same role owns both the Draft submit and this first approve step).
3
Department Head → Pending Finance Approval
Second checkpoint.
4
Accounts Manager → Pending Management Approval
Finance checkpoint.
5
PMO Director → Approved
Final Management sign-off. A Reject at any of the four checkpoints sends the whole document to Rejected, from where the Projects Manager can Revise it back to Draft.

Timesheet Review

Script action · doctype: Timesheet
Not a Frappe Workflow — a lighter status field plus three whitelisted desk actions, because the underlying Timesheet document still needs its own native Draft→Submitted lifecycle alongside this.
1
Employee / Consultant
Logs hours via desk or the /timesheet-entry Web Form. A manday guardrail warns (does not block) if cumulative hours exceed the role's allocated budget.
2
Projects Manager / PMO Director Approve / Reject / Request Changes
Three desk actions on the Timesheet's PMO Review section — Reject and Request Changes both require a reason. This is the fuller 4-action review the PDF specifies, on top of native Timesheet's own binary Draft/Submitted state.

Milestone Approval

Script action · doctype: Task (is_milestone=1)
Only applies to a Task flagged both is_milestone and approval_required — a milestone without that flag can be marked Completed by anyone with normal Task access, no gate.
1
System
Blocks a flagged milestone from reaching Completed until it has been explicitly approved — enforced server-side, not just a UI hint.
2
Projects Manager / PMO Director Approve Milestone
A single desk action stamps who approved it and when; the Task can then move to Completed normally.

Change Request

Script action · doctype: Project Change Request
The one approval in this whole module decided by the client, not by PMO — worth calling out since every other flow above is internal.
1
Projects Manager Draft → Submitted
Logs the scope change; the Impact Calculator estimates extra cost from role rates × additional mandays.
2
Client Portal: Approve / Reject
At /change-request-approval — only that Customer's own portal users can act on it (checked server-side). Approve automatically versions the Project Budget: snapshots the current total into Version History, adds the CR's estimated cost onto the Labor category, and increments the budget version — Reject has no budget effect.

3 · A Day in the Life

Condensed end-to-end paths through the desk/portal, one per role, tying the workflows above into what a real week looks like.

PMO Director

  1. Opens PMO Executive Dashboard — portfolio-wide view: active projects, on-time %, budget vs. actual, risk counts, customer satisfaction (NPS).
  2. Clears the approval queue: new Project Kickoffs, Consultant applications, Project Budgets waiting on the final Management step.
  3. Checks the Risk Heat Map and High Risk Projects report for anything crossing severity thresholds.
  4. Reviews PMO Cockpit Dashboard for manday utilization and missing-logsheet flags across the team.

Projects Manager

  1. Sets up a new engagement: Project → Kickoff Checklist → Deliverables Checklist → role-based budget → Submit for Kickoff Review.
  2. Runs the PMO Task Board Kanban day to day; checks the Gantt's RAG (🟢🟡🔴) schedule-health coloring.
  3. Reviews team timesheets (Approve / Reject / Request Changes) before the Monthly Billing Wizard runs.
  4. Logs and submits any scope changes as a Change Request; drafts the Project Budget and pushes it into the approval chain.
  5. Approves flagged milestones once their deliverables are actually done.

Client (Portal)

  1. Receives the automatic welcome email → logs into Client Kickoff Pack (/client-kickoff-pack) for onboarding docs and the escalation contact matrix.
  2. Gets notified of any Change Request → reviews and approves/rejects at /change-request-approval.
  3. At project close, receives the automatic NPS email → fills the public /project-nps-survey form (0–10 score + feedback).

Consultant / Employee (resourcing)

  1. Consultant: applies at /consultant-signup — profile, skills, BGV/NDA upload, rate card, weekly availability. Waits for PMO Director approval.
  2. Once approved (and, for a Consultant, once a System Manager has created their User), logs hours via desk Timesheet or the mobile /timesheet-entry form.
  3. Gets a weekly reminder email if timesheet hours are missing for the prior week.
  4. May be booked, transferred, or replaced on a project by the Projects Manager via Resource Allocation — no self-service on that part.

4 · Standing Up a Fresh Site

Installing the app is not enough by itself — most of the module's DocTypes, roles, workflows, and Web Forms are created by an idempotent setup script rather than by fixtures, deliberately kept off after_migrate (a brand-new production site lacks Company/Item Group/UOM masters at migrate time, which would make an automatic run fail). This is the real, one-time sequence a System Manager runs.

  1. Install the app: bench --site <site> install-app scale_project_management, then bench --site <site> migrate — this brings in the Custom Fields, Property Setters, the 3 Workflows, the Department Head role, and Reports that are in fixtures.
  2. Make sure Company, a Fiscal Year, and at least one Cost Center exist first (native ERPNext setup — PMO budget tracking depends on Cost Center existing).
  3. Run the schema/setup script once: bench --site <site> execute scale_project_management.demo_setup.run_all — this is what actually creates the PMO Director role, the Consultant/Contractor/Risk/Change Request/Budget DocTypes, the 3 public/portal Web Forms, and the PMO Settings doctype (Consultant and Contractor management both default off).
  4. Optional, for a demo or UAT environment only: bench --site <site> execute scale_project_management.demo_setup.seed_demo_data — walks one sample engagement through every PMO surface so the site isn't empty on first login. Skip this on a real production site.
  5. Create Frappe User accounts for internal staff and assign roles from §1 (PMO Director, Projects Manager, Department Head — Accounts Manager is usually already assigned via Accounts/Finance).
  6. If the client uses external Consultants or Contractors, a PMO Director turns the relevant toggle on in PMO Settings — until then, the public signup form stays unpublished and new profiles of that type can't be created at all.
  7. Configure an outgoing Email Account (Settings → Email Account) — without one, welcome emails, timesheet reminders, and NPS survey triggers all queue silently but never actually send. This is a genuine environment gap on the current dev site (documented repeatedly across T03/T05/T08/T15 testing) — not a code defect.

5 · Known Gaps & Open Items

Everything the plan called for is built (PMO-01 to PMO-20, all ✅ Done per pmo-module.html's Progress Tracker). What's below are the honest loose ends found while re-verifying that status for this manual — not missing code, but decisions, dependencies, or documentation that still need attention.

AI Track not started (PMO-17, 18, 19 + tasks V00–V09). AI Project Delay Prediction, AI Resource Optimization, and AI Budget Forecasting are all ◯ Not Started — this is Veeren's team's scope entirely, tracked separately, not a PMO delivery gap. Downstream effect: the Executive Dashboard's AI Project Health Score, Budget Forecast Accuracy, Resource Capacity Forecast, and Top AI Recommendations tiles (4 of the PDF's 17 named metrics) stay empty until that track lands. The 13 other metrics, including Customer Satisfaction Score (NPS), are live today.
Task status labels don't match the source PDF's wording. Native Task ships Open/Working/Pending Review/Overdue/Completed/Cancelled; the PDF asks for Not Started/In Progress/On Hold/Under Review/Completed/Cancelled — including an "On Hold" state with no native equivalent. Renaming needs a client decision first, since Kanban columns, hooks, and reports currently key off the literal current strings — a silent rename would break all three.
"Project impact" has no single combined score yet. The Project Impact Report (PMO-12) shows Goals, Realized Risks, Budget, and NPS side by side — exactly what the source Excel asks for — but the Excel's own note ("business definition of 'impact' still requested") is still open. Not a build gap; a business definition is needed before a single blended score could be built.
No Program/Portfolio grouping. The PDF's concept of grouping multiple Projects under a Program, and Programs under a Portfolio, has no ERPNext equivalent and isn't in the Excel scope either. Deliberately not built — flagged here in case the business wants project hierarchies above the single-Project level later.
The general ERP Flow & Operations Manual is stale on Projects (PMO). erp-flow-manual.html §6 still marks every PMO addition as "Planned" (purple callouts) from when that section was written on 5 Aug, before the module was built — it hasn't been refreshed since PMO went to 20/20 Done on 19 Aug. Worth a follow-up pass to bring it in line with this page.
Consultant/Contractor onboarding is off by default. Not a bug — an explicit 12 Aug decision — but worth knowing before a client asks "why can't I add a consultant": a fresh site ships with both toggles off in PMO Settings until a PMO Director turns them on (see §4, step 6).
