Worth Knowing Before The Walkthrough

Two Real Problems Caught In The First Feature Alone

Caught During Testing, Fixed Before Anyone Saw Them
  • The approval chain silently failed to save at all the first time — Frappe's workflow states and actions are themselves records in their own master lists, invisibly auto-created by the Desk UI; building the workflow through code directly skipped that step until it was traced down and fixed.
  • The duplicate-requisition warning could name the request itself as a duplicate of itself. Fixed with an explicit self-exclusion check.

Purchase Requisition

🟢 Progressed
A structured way for any department to request materials, services, or assets — with approval, budget awareness, and procurement planning before a purchase ever starts.
Added
  • Department, Priority, Budget Reference, Estimated Value, and Justification captured on every request
  • Full approval chain: Department Head → Purchase Manager → Finance → Management, routing to Management only above a value threshold — tested live on both the low-value and high-value paths, plus a reject path

  • The 4 named reports (Pending Requisitions, Department-wise PR, Budget vs Requested, PR Ageing) aren't built yet

PUR-05

Invoice Matching (3-way)

🟢 Progressed
Purchase Invoice validated against order and receipt — quantity and rate deviations flagged before money moves.
Added
  • Every invoice is automatically checked against its Purchase Order and Goods Receipt on save — a Match Status (Matched / Flagged) and a plain-language deviation note appear right on the invoice, no manual cross-checking three documents by hand
  • Catches two things: the price billed differs from what was ordered/received, or more is being billed than was actually received (billing less than received is normal partial billing, not flagged)
Real Issue Found & Fixed
  • The system already had a native price check, but it was set to hard-stop the invoice outright on any mismatch — the opposite of the "flag it, don't block it" behaviour this feature needed. Reset to a warning, so finance sees the flag but the invoice can still be saved for review

PUR-14

Vendor Portal

🟢 Progressed
Supplier self-service — quotation submission, PO acknowledgment, invoice submission.
Added
  • A real "Acknowledge This Order" page suppliers can use on their own Purchase Orders — timestamped automatically
  • A real "Submit Your Invoice" page — the system did have something that looked similar already, but it was wired so it could never actually be used by an outside vendor (see right)
  • Every submission is checked server-side against the supplier's own account — one vendor can never see or act on another vendor's orders or invoices, tested live with two separate real vendor logins
